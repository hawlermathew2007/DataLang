Privacy Policy of Bright Minds Tuition Centre

We have appointed a Data Protection Officer who is responsible for PDPA compliance. Contact the DPO at dpo@brightminds.example.

We collect your personal data for the following purposes: enrolment, billing and communication about your child's lessons. We collect your personal data only with your consent. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct any error.

We retain student records for two years after the last lesson and then delete them. Our systems use encryption and only authorised staff may access personal data.

Our canteen partner serves lunch from twelve o'clock every weekday.
